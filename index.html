About Bhumi Sure

Bhumi Sure is a proposed AI-powered land record digitization and verification system developed for Smart India Hackathon 2026, Problem Statement 26018.


The planned system combines document digitization, AI-assisted data extraction, validation, confidence scoring, officer review and GIS visualization.
